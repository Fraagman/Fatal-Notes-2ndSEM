# Physics Previous Year Questions (PYQs) - Topic-wise & Year-wise

This document contains a compiled list of Physics and Physics for Computer Engineers questions extracted from the provided past year question papers, sorted by year.

## Year 2025

### Date: 08/12/2025 | Physics for Quantum Computing
* **Q1.** * a) Explain the properties of wave function $\Psi$.
  * b) A photon with momentum $p_{0}=\frac{E_{0}}{c}=1.02~MeV/c$, scattered with an stationary electron and scattered photon momentum becomes $p_{1}=\frac{\epsilon_{1}}{c}=0.255~MeV/c$. Find the scattering angle of the photon.
  * c) Illuminating the surface of the metal cathode, alternately with light of wavelengths $\lambda_{1}=0.35~\mu m$ and $\lambda_{2}=0.54~\mu m$. It was found that corresponding maximum velocities of the photoelectrons differ by a factor $\eta=2$. Solve the work function of the metal cathode.
  * d) A wave function associated with a particle is given by $\Psi=Ae^{-i(\omega t-kx)}$, show that $E\Psi=i\hbar\frac{d\Psi}{dt}$.
  * e) Explain the concept of the Bloch sphere and its role in representing qubit states in quantum computing.
* **Q2.** * a) Explain the Fermi energy? If the fermi distribution function is defined by $f(E)=\frac{1}{e^{\frac{(E-E_{F})}{k_{B}T}}+1}$, find the condition for the fermi function $f(E)=1$.
  * b) Compare the classical and quantum free electron theories.
* **Q3.** Explain the Hall Effect and its physical significance, highlighting key applications of this phenomenon in modern technology.
* **Q4.** Explain how laser technologies are utilized in the fields of security and medicine, highlighting their key applications in each domain.
* **Q5.** Explain, how do the AC and DC Josephson effects differ, and in what ways do they appear in superconducting systems.
